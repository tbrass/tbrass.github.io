Classroom Detective Guide

🕵️‍♂️ Your Mission:

We use different technologies every day, but which ones actually use **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**? Put your detective cap on and sort the cards in the Deck into their correct folders!

🤖 AI Category Uses models, learns patterns, handles raw datasets, predicts predictions dynamically.
⚖️ Maybe / Some AI Features classic systems moving into advanced ones (hybrid rules and language databases).
⚙️ Not AI Category Perfect system of formulas, calculators, clocks, simple hardcoded instruction lines.

👉 How to Play:

  • Drag and Drop: Pull any technology card with your mouse/finger and drop it directly inside a bucket.
  • Easy Tap-to-Place: For laptops, Chromebooks, or mobile tablets, click any card first (it turns gold), then click the target bucket above.
  • Check Answers: Click the green Check Answers button to verify your selections. Correct ones show green, incorrect show red!
  • Click for Secret Explanations: After you check answers, click on any of your sorted cards to read its detailed breakdown!
